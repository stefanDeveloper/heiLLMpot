# Code Of Conduct

heiLLMpot is a defensive security research project. Discussions should stay
professional, evidence-based, and focused on making the software safer and more
useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but ask for clarification when something is ambiguous.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Handle security-sensitive details responsibly.
- Avoid sharing real captured traffic, credentials, or private operational data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Publishing private contact details, secrets, logs, or captured traffic without
  permission
- Encouraging unauthorized deployment or misuse
- Derailing technical discussions with repeated off-topic arguments

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
block participants who violate these guidelines. Security-sensitive reports may
be redirected to the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
